# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation! These resources describe the key processes, artifacts, and roles that OctoAcme uses for effective project delivery.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme employs a comprehensive, cross-functional project management approach centered on five core principles: **customer-first delivery**, **iterative development**, **clear ownership**, **data-informed decisions**, and **psychological safety**. 

Our process follows a structured five-phase lifecycle:
1. **Initiation** — Define problem statements and stakeholders
2. **Planning** — Break work into shippable increments and identify risks
3. **Execution** — Build, test, review, and iterate with daily standups and tracking
4. **Release** — Deploy to production with rigorous pre-release checks and rollback plans
5. **Retrospective** — Capture learnings and drive continuous improvement

### Core Roles & Personas

- **Project Managers (PM)** — Coordinate delivery, manage schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Managers (PdM)** — Define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success
- **Developers** — Implement features, maintain tests, and collaborate on design
- **QA/Testing** — Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders** — Provide inputs, approvals, and strategic direction

### Key Practices

- **Clear ownership** — Every project has named PM and Product Lead
- **Iterative delivery** — Small, testable increments with frequent feedback loops
- **Acceptance criteria & Definition of Done** — Standardized quality gates
- **Risk management** — Proactive identification, assessment, and mitigation
- **Stakeholder communication** — Weekly syncs, monthly updates, and transparent status reporting
- **Quality assurance** — Unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and security scanning
- **Continuous improvement** — Post-sprint retrospectives with tracked action items

### Communication Cadence

- Daily standups (15 min) — Progress, blockers, dependencies
- Weekly PM + PdM sync — Strategy and risk review
- Twice-weekly team standups — Delivery updates (or as agreed)
- Monthly stakeholder updates — High-level status and milestones
- Ad-hoc escalations — When risks or decisions need immediate attention

## Key Artifacts

Every OctoAcme project maintains:
- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Problem statement, goals, scope, and timeline
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — Milestones and delivery schedule
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized work with acceptance criteria
- **Acceptance Criteria & Definition of Done** — Quality gates
- **Risk Register** — Active risks with mitigation plans and owners
- **Retrospective notes and action items** — Learnings and improvements
